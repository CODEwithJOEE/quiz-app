## How to Login

1. Open the app link: `https://your-app.vercel.app` (or your school's custom domain)
2. Enter the **email** and **password** provided by your admin or teacher
3. Tap **"Sign In"**

**⚠️ Note:** There is no **Sign Up** button. Your account is created by an admin or teacher. If you don't have an account, contact them.

## Install on Phone

The app works in a browser, but it's **better when installed** — faster, and it has fullscreen mode.

### Android (Chrome)

1. Open the app link in Chrome
2. Tap the menu (⋮) at the top
3. Tap **"Install app"** or **"Add to Home screen"**
4. Tap **"Install"**
5. The icon appears on your home screen

### iPhone (Safari)

1. Open the app link in **Safari** (⚠️ not Chrome)
2. Tap the **Share** button (⎋) at the bottom
3. Scroll down → tap **"Add to Home Screen"**
4. Tap **"Add"**
5. The icon appears on your home screen

### Why Install?

- ✅ Opens fast (like a native app)
- ✅ Fullscreen mode — no browser controls
- ✅ More secure for quiz-taking (harder to switch apps)
- ✅ Auto-updates

## Navigation

At the bottom of the screen is the **bottom navigation**:

| Icon | Label         | Function                              | Visible to       |
| ---- | ------------- | ------------------------------------- | ---------------- |
| 🏠   | **Home**      | Dashboard, invitations, quick actions | All roles        |
| 🚪   | **Rooms**     | All your rooms                        | Teacher, Student |
| 📋   | **Quiz**      | Quiz listing                          | Teacher, Student |
| 🎓   | **Students**  | Manage students                       | Teacher only     |
| ⚙️   | **Dashboard** | System overview + admin tools         | Super Admin only |
| 👤   | **Profile**   | Account info, dark mode, logout, help | All roles        |

**Note:** The nav items you see depend on your role.

**For super admins:** The nav shows **Dashboard**, not "Users". You reach user management from the Dashboard's Quick Links or stat cards.

## Dark Mode

The app supports **dark mode**:

1. Go to the **Profile** page
2. In the **"Appearance"** section, choose:
   - **Light** — light theme
   - **Dark** — dark theme
   - **System** — follows your phone settings

Your preference is saved on your device.

## Common Issues

**I can't log in**

- Check your email spelling
- Passwords are case-sensitive
- If you forgot it, contact your admin (for teachers) or teacher (for students)

**I don't see any rooms**

- If you're a student: you need to **accept the invitation** first
- Go to the **Home** page → look for "Room Invitations"

**The app icon doesn't show up**

- iPhone: use **Safari** (not Chrome)
- Android: use **Chrome** (not Firefox)
- Try restarting the browser

**The quiz froze while I was taking it**

- Don't close the app
- Refresh the browser (pull down on mobile)
- Your progress is saved
- If it still doesn't work, contact your teacher

**I was terminated during a quiz**

- Check the **Result page** for the reason
- Common cause: switching tabs 3 times
- Contact your teacher if you disagree

**My score shows "Pending Grading"**

- The quiz has essay questions your teacher hasn't graded yet
- Your MCQ points are already counted
- Wait — the score updates automatically once your teacher grades

## Tips

- **Install the app** before an exam
- **Charge your phone** before taking a quiz
- **Connect to stable WiFi** or mobile data
- **Turn on Do Not Disturb** during a quiz (to avoid notification-triggered violations)
- **Close other apps** for smooth quiz-taking
